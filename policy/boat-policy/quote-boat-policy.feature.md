# Quote Boat Policy

## Basic boat quote @v1 [proposed]

- **Given** a prospect provides the boat's length, motor type, hull value, and mooring location
- **When** the prospect requests a quote
- **Then** an annual premium is quoted with hull and liability coverage listed separately

## Winter lay-up discount @v1 [proposed]

- **Given** the boat is declared out of the water from November through March
- **When** the premium is calculated
- **Then** a 20% lay-up discount is applied
- **And** the policy notes that no navigation coverage applies during the lay-up period
